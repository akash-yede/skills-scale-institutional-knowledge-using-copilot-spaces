# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

OctoAcme uses a structured, iterative approach to project management that emphasizes **customer value**, **clear ownership**, and **data-driven decisions**. Our processes are designed to enable consistent, repeatable project execution while maintaining transparency and psychological safety across all team members.

### Core Principles
- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability in all decisions
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments regularly
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has a named Project Manager (PM) and Product Lead
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback, learning, and continuous improvement

## Project Lifecycle

OctoAcme projects follow five key phases from inception to continuous improvement:

1. **Initiation** – Validate business need, identify stakeholders, and gain alignment on success metrics
2. **Planning** – Define scope, resources, dependencies, milestones, and create an actionable backlog
3. **Execution** – Build, test, review, iterate, and track progress toward milestones
4. **Release** – Deploy to production safely with pre-release checks, smoke tests, and rollback procedures
5. **Close & Retrospective** – Capture learnings, document improvements, and drive continuous enhancement

## Core Roles

- **Project Manager (PM)**: Coordinates delivery, manages schedules, risks, and communications
- **Product Manager (PdM)**: Defines outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, and measures success
- **Developers**: Implement features, collaborate on design and testability
- **QA/Testing**: Validate quality and acceptance criteria
- **Stakeholders**: Provide inputs, approvals, and strategic direction

## Key Artifacts

Across all phases, OctoAcme projects maintain:
- **Project Charter / One-pager** – Problem, goal, success metrics, and stakeholders
- **Roadmap and Release Plan** – Timeline and key milestones
- **Prioritized Backlog** – With acceptance criteria and estimates
- **Risk Register** – Tracking impact, likelihood, and mitigation
- **Retrospective Notes** – Documenting learnings and action items
- **Project Board** – Visual status tracking (Backlog → Ready → In Progress → In Review → QA → Done)

Process documentation should be:
- **Reviewed quarterly** for relevance and accuracy

- **Referenced in Copilot Spaces** so institutional knowledge is easily accessible

## Communication Cadence

- **Daily standups** (15 min) – Progress, blockers, dependencies
- **Weekly PM + PdM sync** – Alignment and decision-making
- **Twice-weekly delivery team standups** – Execution coordination (as agreed by team)
- **Weekly stakeholder updates** – Status, risks, asks
- **Monthly stakeholder briefings** – Strategic updates
- **Sprint/milestone retrospectives** – Learnings and improvements
- **Ad-hoc escalations** – For blockers and critical issues
